CAMPUS TOPOGRAPHY & RESILIENCE

The 4 Railway Underpasses of VIT Vellore

The Katpadi railway line physically bisects VIT Vellore into Hostels (North) and Academic Blocks (South). These 4 underpasses are the only pedestrian arteries connecting campus.

DRY & PASSABLE CANAL CULVERT

Central Canal Underpass

MT Block / TT Annexe ↔ Just Bake / E & K Block

Low-clearance canal culvert with a 2.4m topographic elevation depression. Highly susceptible to storm water accumulation during cloudbursts.

Auto-reroutes pedestrians to H&J Block Tunnel during downpours. View on Map ↗
DRY & PASSABLE PERIMETER CULVERT

West Boundary Canal Underpass

Anna Audi Courts ↔ A-Block Hostels / One Food World

Perimeter drainage underpass adjoining the western boundary. Runoff from adjacent fields quickly submerges this low-dip crossing.

Alerts western hostel residents to use the central arterial crossings. View on Map ↗
SAFE HIGH-WATER ARTERY REINFORCED TUNNEL

H & J Block Main Tunnel

Technology Tower / Fountain ↔ H & J Hostels

Reinforced, high-clearance arterial tunnel equipped with dedicated storm pumps. Engineered to remain open and safe during severe monsoon downpours.

Primary recommended bypass corridor for north-south pedestrian crossing. View on Map ↗
HIGH-RESILIENCE ARTERY HIGHWAY CROSSING

Pearl Research Park (PRP) Tunnel

Main Campus Road ↔ Gate 11A / Pearl Research Park

Grade-separated multi-lane arterial road underpass connecting Gate 11A and the Pearl Research Park courtyard. Elevated gradient prevents waterlogging.

High-throughput corridor connecting the eastern academic district. View on Map ↗
